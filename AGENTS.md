# Project rules

- Billing display and checkout recovery share the pure subscription-state module; missing customers with protected subscription access must fail closed, and every customer replacement is logged. Why: recovery must not create duplicate paid subscriptions or contradict access state.

- Task checklists live on the series master (`appointments.checklist`); completions store a snapshot in `checklist_result`, and NULL means "no checklist data", never "unticked". Why: history must survive later edits to the checklist, and overrides don't carry their own copy.
- Guided routine suggestions come from `src/lib/care-needs/routine-templates.ts`. What was created is tracked in `children.care_needs.settings.scaffolds[cap].routines`, separate from the instruction-offer `status`. Why: re-opening the page or resetting suggestions must never duplicate tasks.
- Notification sweeps must page large reads (`fetchAllPages`) and chunk `.in()` lookups (`fetchInChunks`, at most 100 ids). If an override lookup fails, skip those masters for that run. Why: the 1000-row API cap and missing override data would silently drop pushes or push cancelled occurrences.
- Recurring tasks are expanded only by `expandRecurrence` in `src/lib/schedule/recurrence.ts`, in the family timezone (families.timezone, default Europe/Stockholm), by both the app and the push sweep; `useAppointments` stays disabled until that timezone is known and its ranges are family-tz midnights. Why: app and server must produce identical `occurrence_at` or completions don't match and false "missed" pushes fire.
- Appointment writes: owner, or creator; occurrence overrides follow the series creator (private.can_manage_appointment). family_id/recurrence_parent_id are immutable and created_by is preserved by trigger; client mirrors this with canManageAppointment. Why: UI and database must agree on who can change a task.
- Task sweep occurrences come from expandMasterForSweep (src/lib/schedule/sweep-occurrences.ts): key = original slot (recurrence_override_at), time = override starts_at; thresholds come from the master. Why: completions and dedupe match on the original slot while pushes must follow the moved time shown on Today.
